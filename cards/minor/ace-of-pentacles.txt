**Upright:**
New luck, good health or work for new prosperity. There is no desire to burn, flow or fly in the Ace of Pentacles; here is a physical need to be grounded, to make, build and touch. Like a seed in the ground, there is a desire to sprout roots and just become.

The pentacle is taken in hand, and, for now, the querent wants only to have it solidly in his pocket.


**Reversed:**
They should feel that their luck is turning, the beginnings of renewed health - but it doesn't.

It could indicate a failed anticipation - the querent anticipated a hint that money would be coming his way, and there's no such hint. Things remain speculative, rather than real and solid.
