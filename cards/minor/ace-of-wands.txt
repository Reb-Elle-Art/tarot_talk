**Upright:**
A new spark of energy for a new passion.

The Ace of Wands usually indicates that the querent has just discovered or wants to discover a new religion, philosophy, cause or career choice, possibly even a new sexual partner.

They feel their energy go up, they feel the heat, and they want more of it. They take up the wand and start walking.


**Reversed:**
Reversed Ace of Wands is like trying to start a fire, and getting nothing. No smoke, no spark. They should be feeling excited at a new prospect or ambition, but don't.

It's the hope of a hope that never comes. There's not even the feeling that something was there and died - it was never there in the first place.
